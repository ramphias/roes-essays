# 当智能变得廉价，什么会变得稀缺？
# What Becomes Scarce When Intelligence Becomes Cheap?

如果 AI 最终让"智能"本身从稀缺资源变成一种廉价、近乎无限供给的生产要素，稀缺性和经济价值会向哪里迁移？谁控制新的稀缺资源，谁可能获得这一轮生产力革命最大的长期收益？或者，谁可以在迁移过程中获利，尽管这个窗口期可能会非常短。

**If AI ultimately turns intelligence itself from a scarce resource into a cheap, near-limitless factor of production, where do scarcity — and economic value with it — migrate? Who controls the new scarce resources, and who might capture the largest long-term gains from this round of the productivity revolution? Or who can profit from the migration itself, even though the window may be very short.**

工业革命放大了机器、资本与规模化组织的价值；互联网放大了入口、数据、平台与注意力的价值。AI 可能再次改变稀缺性的分布。当生成、分析和决策能力越来越便宜，五类资源值得关注：

**The Industrial Revolution amplified the value of machines, capital, and large-scale organization; the internet amplified entry points, data, platforms, and attention. AI may shift the distribution of scarcity again. As generation, analysis, and decision-making grow cheaper, five resources deserve attention:**

## 需求

当生产能力极大丰富之后，问题从"生产什么"转向"谁需要这些东西"，甚至是"如何生产需求"，但这一点和互联网革命有类似之处，也许只是互联网革命的升级版本。

**Demand**

**Once productive capacity becomes abundant, the question shifts from *what to produce* to *who needs it* — and even to *how demand is produced*. This resembles the internet revolution; it may be merely an upgraded version of it rather than something new.**

## 信任

当内容、产品、Agent、建议乃至数字身份都可以大规模生成时，判断"谁值得相信"会越来越昂贵。品牌、声誉、身份认证、历史记录、责任承担、担保和保险，都可能因此升值，AI一定会有自己的身份体系，也许会有自己的保险和再保险公司。

**Trust**

**When content, products, agents, advice, and even digital identities can all be generated at scale, judging whom to trust grows ever more expensive. Brand, reputation, identity verification, track record, liability, guarantees, and insurance all stand to appreciate. AI will have its own identity system, and perhaps its own insurers and reinsurers.**

## 判断与选择

AI 可以一分钟产生过去一个团队一年才能提出的方案，因此未来真正稀缺的是判断哪一个方案值得做。Generation 越便宜，Selection 越昂贵。

**Judgment and Selection**

**AI can produce in a minute what it once took a team a year to propose. What is genuinely scarce, then, is judging which proposal is worth doing. The cheaper Generation becomes, the more expensive Selection becomes.**

## 前沿发现

答案越来越便宜，发现真正值得解决的新问题和能力越来越重要，当然这一点自人类诞生之初就一直稀缺，只是生产力没有提升之前只是一件重要而不紧急的事情，如果AI能带来生产力的飞跃，也许新药，新材料，星辰大海会真的实现。

**Frontier Discovery**

**Answers are getting cheaper, and discovering genuinely new problems and capabilities worth solving is getting more important. This, of course, has been scarce since the beginning of humanity — until productivity improved it was merely an important-but-not-urgent matter. If AI delivers a leap in productivity, then new drugs, new materials, and the stars and the boundless sea may actually come within reach.**

## 现实稀缺

土地、能源、自然资源、地理位置、基础设施、牌照、顶级制造能力以及人的时间无法无限复制，不过这一点并不是新的稀缺。

**Physical Scarcity**

**Land, energy, natural resources, geography, infrastructure, licenses, top-tier manufacturing capacity, and human time cannot be replicated without limit — though this is not new scarcity.**

分配与触达能力也许也会持续稀缺，我还没有想清楚，也许还有情绪价值，类似于第一点"需求"，看上去也像是互联网革命的升级版。

**Distribution and access may also remain scarce. I have not thought this through; perhaps there is also emotional value — much like the first point, demand — which again looks like an upgraded version of the internet revolution.**

而未来最有潜力的商业机会应该是在这些稀缺性的交叉点上，例如需求x信任是Personal Agent，信任x判断是Enterprise Agent或Decisioning Engine，需求x前沿发现是新科技公司。

**The most promising commercial opportunities should sit at the intersections of these scarce resources**: demand × trust is the Personal Agent; trust × judgment is the Enterprise Agent or Decisioning Engine; demand × frontier discovery is the new technology company.

## 收束

如果 Intelligence 的边际成本持续下降，下一代经济体系究竟会围绕哪些稀缺资源重新组织？不仅仅是再做一个"AI + X"的生产力工具，谁可以定义新的市场、新的产权结构或新的交易机制，让这些新的稀缺资源第一次可以被识别 → 记录 → 量化 → 定价 → 交易 → 复利，这可能才是真正意义上的"生产关系创新"。

**Conclusion**

**If the marginal cost of intelligence keeps falling, which scarce resources will the next economic system reorganize itself around? It is not enough to build yet another "AI + X" productivity tool. Whoever can define the new markets, the new property-rights structures, or the new mechanisms of exchange — so that these new scarce resources can, for the first time, be **identified → recorded → quantified → priced → traded → compounded** — may be doing what genuinely counts as innovation in the relations of production.**
